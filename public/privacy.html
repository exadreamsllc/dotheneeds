Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 9, 2026 · Version: v1.0-2026 · Operated by EXADreams LLC

We do not sell your data. We do not run advertising. Your declaration is yours. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Who This Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of DoTheNeeds (dotheneeds.com), a product of EXADreams LLC, Frisco, Texas, USA. It covers data collected when you visit the Platform, read declarations, or submit your own declaration.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Display your declaration on the public wall (if not anonymous)
  • Compute stage and scope for your cause (aggregated, not personal)
  • Find matches between your declaration and others (text similarity)
  • Send notifications if you provided an email address and opted in
  • Moderate content to remove prohibited declarations
  • Aggregate reporting — anonymised data about what the world wants to change, shared with NGO or research partners with no personal identifiers
  • Legal compliance — complying with applicable law, court orders, or regulatory requests

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, behavioural tracking, or sale to third parties.

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR (EU/UK) and CCPA (California), you have the following rights:

  • Right to access — request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal information. On erasure: your name, age, nationality, residence, and email are permanently deleted. Your hash is retained (no personal data attached) as proof of existence.
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to portability — request your declaration data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to any processing you haven't consented to
  • Right to opt out of data sale — we do not sell data. There is nothing to opt out of.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@dotheneeds.com with your Declaration ID.

6. Data Retention

  • Active declarations — retained indefinitely as long as the Platform operates
  • Removed declarations — personal fields deleted within 30 days; hash retained permanently
  • Email addresses — retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion
  • Policy agreement records — retained for 7 years (legal compliance)
  • Moderation logs — retained for 2 years

7. Security

We use the following security measures:

  • All data in transit protected by TLS 1.3
  • Database hosted on Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS) enabled
  • Service role keys stored only in Cloudflare's encrypted secrets — never in code
  • Email addresses stored separately from public-facing declaration data
  • No full IP addresses stored at any point

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has submitted a declaration, contact us at privacy@dotheneeds.com and we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy as our practices change. The version number and effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the current version. We will notify registered users by email of material changes.
