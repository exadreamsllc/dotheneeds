N
Nathan, Founder — EXADreams LLC Frisco, Texas · Health & Wellbeing + Economy & Finance
🛠 Doer — committed to action
"I want health insurance to be peer-to-peer — a community contribution pool, governed transparently, where the only relationship that truly matters is between a patient and a doctor.

Today's middlemen exist because the system was built before automation. Tomorrow's system doesn't need them in the same form. Technology can replace the bureaucracy, build the guardrails, and redirect every dollar spent on administration back into actual care.

If your premium goes down and your benefits improve — don't you want that?

If you can't imagine the system changing, this conversation isn't for you. But if you can — let's figure out the how together. The what is clear. The how is for the builders, the policymakers, the doers. That conversation starts here."
My move I built DoTheNeeds.com — a platform to turn individual voices into movements. Because this conversation needed to start somewhere, and it starts here. That makes me a doer, not just a talker. This page is my contribution.

DoTheNeeds was built because one conversation is never enough — but a movement of a thousand conversations can change a system. This is what triggered it. It can't be a voice. It has to be a movement.
